============================================================
LUPUS NEPHRITIS scRNA-seq — BIOLOGICAL INTERPRETATION
============================================================

OVERALL MODEL
HL and HS appear to represent distinct immune states rather than
simply differences in immune-cell abundance.

HL is relatively lymphocyte/remodeling-heavy, with strong
cell-intrinsic transcriptional/epigenetic remodeling across
T-cell, NK and related lymphocyte compartments.

HS is relatively myeloid/DC inflammatory-heavy, with strong
cytokine, interferon and inflammatory signaling programs.

============================================================
HL BIOLOGY
============================================================

1. LYMPHOCYTE-INTRINSIC REMODELING
Strong HL-predominant DE is present in several lymphocyte populations,
including Naive CD8 T, Naive T/TCF7+, Activated CD4 T, NK and Treg.

This suggests that the HL-vs-HS difference is not explained solely
by changes in cell composition; lymphocytes themselves show altered
transcriptional states.

2. CYTOTOXIC CD8 T
Cytotoxic CD8 T showed 17 HL-associated versus 3 HS-associated
DE genes in the earlier cell-level summary.

HL-associated GSEA included strong chromatin-remodeling and
cytoplasmic-translation programs.

Important HL genes included TESK1, CTBP2, CENPB, MBD2, SAMD1,
CYHR1, FOXK2, FBRSL1, MICAL2 and others.

However, Cytotoxic CD8 T also showed an HS-enriched adaptive
immune-response GSEA pathway (NES approximately -1.68,
adjusted p approximately 6.8e-4).

Therefore this compartment should NOT be described as purely HL.
It appears to contain mixed/complex state remodeling.

3. CHROMATIN / EPIGENETIC STATE
Chromatin-remodeling was one of the strongest HL-enriched GSEA
signals in Cytotoxic CD8 T.

This supports a hypothesis of altered transcriptional/epigenetic
state between HL and HS.

4. TRANSLATION / PROTEIN SYNTHESIS
Cytoplasmic translation and related protein-synthesis programs
were repeatedly enriched toward HL in the observed GSEA results.

This may indicate altered cellular activity or protein-production
state, but should be validated using the leading-edge genes.

============================================================
HS BIOLOGY
============================================================

1. MYELOID / DC INFLAMMATORY STATE
The strongest coherent HS signal is observed in myeloid and DC
compartments.

CD1C+ DC showed strong HS enrichment for:
- response to cytokine
- cytokine-mediated signaling
- inflammatory response
- defense response
- cytokine production
- chemokine response

CD14+/CD163+ macrophage also showed HS inflammatory/cytokine
programs.

2. IFN-ACTIVATED DC
IFN-activated DC showed strong HS enrichment for:
- cytokine-mediated signaling
- regulation of inflammatory response
- type II interferon response
- canonical NF-kappaB signaling
- inflammatory response
- pyroptotic inflammatory response

This is currently one of the strongest and most biologically
coherent HS-associated signals in the dataset.

3. ADAPTIVE IMMUNE RESPONSE
Cytotoxic CD8 T also showed an HS-enriched adaptive immune-response
pathway.

This suggests that HS may not simply represent a myeloid state;
there may also be coordinated adaptive immune activation/state
changes.

============================================================
LN INTERPRETATION — CURRENT WORKING MODEL
============================================================

The current data support a working model in which the two conditions
represent different immune states in lupus nephritis.

HL:
relative lymphocyte-associated transcriptional/remodeling state,
with strong chromatin/translation signals in Cytotoxic CD8 T and
substantial HL-predominant DE across lymphocyte populations.

HS:
relative inflammatory myeloid/DC state characterized by cytokine,
interferon, NF-kappaB and inflammatory programs.

This interpretation is currently stronger at the level of immune
STATE than at the level of a specific molecular mechanism.

============================================================
IMPORTANT CAUTIONS
============================================================

1. Composition and cell-intrinsic expression must remain separate.
2. GSEA GO terms are highly redundant and should be collapsed into
   biological modules rather than counted as independent findings.
3. Terms such as myoblast fusion, syncytium formation, axon guidance
   and neuron development should NOT be interpreted literally.
4. Leading-edge/core-enrichment genes must be inspected before
   assigning mechanisms to unusual GO terms.
5. Small cell populations require cautious interpretation.
6. DE/GSEA discordance is informative but requires validation.
7. This is a working LN biological model, not yet a final mechanism.
