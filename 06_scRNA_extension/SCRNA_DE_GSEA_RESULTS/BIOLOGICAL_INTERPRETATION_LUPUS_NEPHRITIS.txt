============================================================
BIOLOGICAL INTERPRETATION — LUPUS NEPHRITIS scRNA-seq
HL vs HS
============================================================

DATASET / ANALYSIS STATUS
-------------------------
Single-cell RNA-seq differential expression was performed
within cell types using pseudobulk-level analysis.
There are 4 HL samples and 15 HS samples for almost all
cell types.

DE was followed by GO enrichment and ranked-gene GSEA.

GSEA validation:
Total significant GSEA pathways: 832
HL-enriched pathways: 431
HS-enriched pathways: 401
Cell types represented in GSEA: 22

============================================================
MAIN BIOLOGICAL INTERPRETATION
============================================================

1. HL-ASSOCIATED PROGRAMS
-------------------------
HL-enriched GSEA shows repeated enrichment of:

- chromatin remodeling
- epigenetic regulation / heterochromatin organization
- cytoplasmic translation
- ribosome biogenesis / ribosomal assembly
- cellular differentiation / developmental programs

The most convincing interpretation is therefore that HL cells
show altered transcriptional/epigenetic state together with
changes in translational machinery.

These findings should be interpreted as altered cellular state
rather than as literal activation of developmental or muscle
biology.

Several muscle/myoblast/embryonic-development GO terms are
repeatedly enriched in HL. Because these terms occur across
immune cell types and are biologically unexpected in lupus
immune cells, they should NOT be treated as primary biological
conclusions without leading-edge gene inspection.

These terms may reflect GO redundancy, pleiotropic genes,
shared developmental annotations, or technical/annotation
effects.

============================================================
2. HS-ASSOCIATED PROGRAMS
============================================================

HS shows a much stronger inflammatory/immune-signaling signal
in antigen-presenting and myeloid populations.

Major recurring themes include:

- cytokine response
- cytokine-mediated signaling
- inflammatory response
- regulation of cytokine production
- defense response
- response to chemokines
- type II interferon response
- canonical NF-kappaB signaling
- pyroptotic inflammatory response

The strongest and most coherent HS signal is therefore an
inflammatory myeloid/DC state, particularly involving CD1C+ DC,
CD14+/CD163+ macrophages and IFN-activated DC.

============================================================
3. LUPUS NEPHRITIS CONTEXT
============================================================

In the lupus nephritis context, the key biological question is
whether HL and HS represent different immune-state programs.

The current results suggest a possible contrast between:

HL:
  cellular-state / epigenetic / translational remodeling

versus

HS:
  inflammatory cytokine / interferon / NF-kappaB activity
  particularly in antigen-presenting and myeloid populations.

This is a hypothesis-generating interpretation, not yet a
mechanistic conclusion.

============================================================
4. IMPORTANT T-CELL SIGNAL
============================================================

T-cell populations show substantial differential expression
and GSEA activity, particularly Naive CD8 T, Naive T/TCF7+,
cytotoxic T-cell and gamma-delta T populations.

These should be examined separately for:

- cytotoxicity
- T-cell activation
- exhaustion/dysfunction
- interferon response
- TCR signaling
- metabolic remodeling
- differentiation/state transitions.

============================================================
5. WHAT IS CURRENTLY STRONG
============================================================

Strongest coherent observation:
HS-associated inflammatory/cytokine/IFN programs in myeloid/DC
populations.

Second strong observation:
HL-associated chromatin/epigenetic and translational programs.

Potentially interesting but currently requiring validation:
HL-associated muscle/developmental/myoblast GO terms.

============================================================
6. WHAT MUST NOT BE OVERCLAIMED
============================================================

Do NOT conclude that:
- immune cells are undergoing muscle differentiation;
- lupus nephritis directly activates myoblast pathways;
- developmental GO terms represent a disease mechanism.

These terms require leading-edge gene inspection and preferably
independent pathway-level validation.
